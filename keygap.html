About KeyGap

Find the gaps in your keyboard shortcuts.

Why this exists

Every platform has unclaimed modifier combinations, but finding them means cross-referencing the OS, your browser, your editor, your chat app and everything else you run. KeyGap does that for you.

The main insight: on macOS, ⌘⌃ (Cmd+Ctrl) is almost entirely unclaimed. On Windows, Win+Ctrl is the equivalent. Most people don't know this.

How to use it

Pick your platform and the apps you use. Each tab is a modifier combination. Teal keys are free to claim for your own shortcuts, scripts, Raycast commands or automations. To check one shortcut, choose Check a shortcut by pressing it and press it.

A note on accuracy

Shortcut data comes from official documentation. Some apps let you rebind everything, and plugins or extensions add their own shortcuts. If you spot something wrong, open an issue or pull request.

Use KeyGap with Claude

KeyGap is also an MCP server, so you can ask Claude about free shortcuts without opening this page.

Server URL

https://keygap.app/mcp

Add it to Claude

In Claude, open Settings → Connectors, choose Add custom connector and paste the URL.

In Claude Code, run:

claude mcp add --transport http keygap https://keygap.app/mcp

Tools

  • get_available_keys lists the free keys for a modifier combination, given your apps.
  • check_key checks one shortcut and says what uses it.
  • suggest_hotkeys suggests the best free shortcuts for your setup.
